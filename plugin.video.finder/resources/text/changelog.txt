Version 1.0.02-RC1 (Fork of Forge 2.13)

🗓️ Release History:

• Initial Release: June 30, 2026 — Kodi (v22) Piers Beta 1 (Version 1.0.0)

• Current Release: October 1, 2026 — Kodi (v22) Piers RC-1

🚀 Key Upgrades & Platform Support

• Cross-Platform Compatibility: Fully compatible with all major platforms (including Google TV, Android TV, and LibreELEC). The add-on is officially optimized and ready for the final release of Kodi 22.

• Repository-Driven Updates: Removed the built-in updater to eliminate conflicts with Kodi’s native repository installer. Future updates will now deliver seamlessly direct from my repo.
PunchPlay Integration

• Full List Support: Integrated PunchPlay as a primary tracker and lists provider. Watchlists, Collections, and Personal Lists are fully functional and hosted alongside TMDb and Trakt.

• Database Repair Tool: Added a database repair utility under the maintenance tools. This ensures your movies and shows sync and render correctly if an initial scrape fails.

• Quick Add Functionality: Easily add or remove movies and TV shows from your PunchPlay Watchlist.
	• Setup: Navigate to Finder Addon > Content Section (at the bottom). Enable Quick Add in the context menu settings and adjust the order to your preference (recommended as 1st or 2nd priority).

• Tracked Progress: The In Progress and Next Up shelves are dynamically tied to your actively selected tracker. Find them neatly organized at the bottom of the main TV Show menu.


🛠️ Future Roadmap

• Kodi Library Integration: Upcoming incremental updates will introduce two-way communication and library synchronization. This will sync tracking data with Trakt or PunchPlay, alongside list-only support for TMDb.

• PunchPlay Manager: The dedicated PunchPlay Manager Context Menu will be finalized and rolled out in the next release to expand quick-action options.


🛑 Features Deferred or Excluded

• Simkl Integration: Excluded from current development. The implementation is currently too complex to justify the effort based on the core needs of my immediate users.

• MDBlist.com Integration (On Hold): Development is paused until minor bugs are resolved and authentication methods improve. While the platform shows great promise and I continue to support Linas's excellent work, account security and interface limitations mean it will not be added until a future date.

🔒 Configuration & Developer Notes

• Custom API Keys: You can add your personal API keys for Trakt, TMDb, and PunchPlay by editing the settings_schema.json file located in resources/data/. My testing keys are included for initial functionality, but replacing them with your own is highly recommended for stability.

• Scraper Recommendations: The fork supports most popular scrapers. Magneto is recommended and comes pre-configured in the settings.

• Credits: Special thanks to silencebob2 for his work on Forge. The implementation of the JSON settings schema makes editing incredibly efficient and keeps the add-on feeling light and responsive.
