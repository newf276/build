Finder can use one of three sources as its Primary Tracker: Finder, Trakt or PunchPlay.

Your Primary Tracker controls the watched/progress database used by Finder for features such as watched indicators, In Progress and Next Episodes.

FINDER
- Uses Finder's local watched database.
- No external tracker authorization is required.

TRAKT
- Uses your Trakt watched information.
- Trakt authorization is required.

PUNCHPLAY
- Uses PunchPlay watched history.
- PunchPlay authorization is required.
- Finder rebuilds its PunchPlay watched database when needed and validates the available PunchPlay list data.

Choose carefully when first setting up Finder. If you change tracker services later, allow Finder time to rebuild/synchronize the relevant data before judging the resulting progress lists.
