Finder can use one of three sources as its Primary Tracker: Finder, Trakt or PunchPlay.

Your Primary Tracker controls the watched/progress database used by Finder for features such as watched indicators, In Progress and Next Episodes.

FINDER
- Uses Finder's local watched database.
- No external tracker authorization is required.

TRAKT
- Uses your Trakt watched information.
- Trakt authorization is required.

PUNCHPLAY
- Uses PunchPlay watched history.
- PunchPlay authorization is required.
- When PunchPlay is selected for first use, Finder synchronizes watched history and validates the available Watchlist, Collection and Personal List data.
- Repair PunchPlay Watched Database remains available under maintenance tools if the local watched-state database ever needs rebuilding.

Choose carefully when first setting up Finder. If you change tracker services later, allow Finder time to rebuild or synchronize the relevant data before judging the resulting progress lists.
