Finder provides separate controls for result sorting, filtering and visual highlights.

Result highlights can be based on:
- Provider
- Quality
- Single Color

When Provider or Quality highlighting is selected, the relevant color settings become available.

Sorting and Filtering also includes controls for preferred filters, quality/result limits, seeders for uncached results, prerelease handling and media attributes such as HEVC, HDR, Dolby Vision, AV1, 3D and enhanced/upscaled sources.
