Finder includes playback controls for skip markers such as intros, recaps and outros, plus stinger handling.

Skip marker settings can be enabled separately for intro, recap and outro segments. Finder can use chapter information where available and can apply confidence/submission thresholds so weak markers are not automatically trusted. AnimeSkip support is also available as a source for anime episode skip data.

Playback controls should be enabled gradually so you can confirm they behave as expected with your sources.
