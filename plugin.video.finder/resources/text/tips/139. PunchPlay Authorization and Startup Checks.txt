When PunchPlay is selected or used, Finder validates the PunchPlay account during startup. Finder can report authorization status and load the associated Watchlist, Collection and Personal List information.

If PunchPlay authorization is incomplete, list-management features that require the account will not operate. If watched history cannot be loaded, use Repair PunchPlay Watched Database after confirming authorization.

This validation is intended to make authorization problems visible early rather than allowing them to appear later as missing lists or progress data.
