When PunchPlay is selected or used, Finder validates the PunchPlay account during startup. Finder can report authorization status and load the associated watched history, Watchlist, Collection and Personal List information.

When PunchPlay is chosen as the Primary Tracker during first setup, Finder keeps the first-run sequence open while authorization and the initial watched-state/list synchronization complete. After PunchPlay is ready, Finder completes the Finder Skin handoff and opens the fresh Home screen. At that point the first-run setup is complete and the populated progress widgets can be used immediately; a Kodi restart is not required.

Normal first-run setup should not require the Repair function. If PunchPlay authorization is incomplete, account-dependent list-management features will not operate. If watched history later becomes damaged or cannot be loaded, confirm authorization and then use Repair PunchPlay Watched Database.

This validation is intended to make authorization problems visible early rather than allowing them to appear later as missing lists or progress data.
