Finder supports several recommendation methods. The current Recommendation Service choices are:

- Recommended (TMDb)
- More Like This (IMDb)
- Similar (AI)
- Related (Trakt)

You can also choose how many recently watched items are used as the recommendation seed, from the last 1 through the last 10 watched items.

The exact results will vary by service and by the available metadata/history, so changing the recommendation method can produce noticeably different lists.
