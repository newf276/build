Easynews playback can require special handling on some setups. Finder provides a Playback Method setting under the Easynews settings.

NONE
- Use the normal Easynews playback path.

RETRY
- Retry the Easynews link after a failed first attempt. This can be slower and may not suit every device.

NO SEEK
- Tell Kodi the stream is not seekable. This can make resolution faster and more reliable, but seeking outside the currently cached data is restricted.

BOTH
- Combine NO SEEK with a retry.

Start with NONE and change the method only if Easynews playback on your device benefits from it.
