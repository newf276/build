Finder can work with TMDb Watchlist and TMDb Favorites when the required TMDb account/list access is configured.

TMDb Watchlist and Favorites each have their own sorting controls:
- Title
- Release Date ascending
- Release Date descending
- Shuffle
- Default from TMDb

These lists are separate from Trakt and PunchPlay lists. Use Quick Add or the appropriate manager/context action to work with them where supported.
