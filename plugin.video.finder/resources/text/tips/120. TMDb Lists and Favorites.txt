Finder can work with TMDb Watchlist and TMDb Favorites when the required TMDb account/list access is configured.

TMDb Watchlist and Favorites each have their own sorting controls:
- Title
- Release Date ascending
- Release Date descending
- Shuffle
- Default from TMDb

These lists are separate from Trakt and PunchPlay lists. Use Quick Add or the appropriate manager/context action to work with them where supported.

If TMDb is signed out, Finder -> My Lists shows Authorize TMDb. Select it and answer Yes to use the existing browser/QR sign-in flow, or No to decline. Authorized accounts show TMDb Lists instead, with an Authorize TMDb entry inside for manual reauthorization. Leave and reopen My Lists after sign-in to refresh its contents. The authorization site may still identify the inherited application as Forge until a Finder application registration is configured.
