Finder includes a manual Trakt <-> PunchPlay Transfer utility when both accounts are authorized. Open Tools -> Trakt ↔ PunchPlay Transfer.

You can choose a scope:
- Watchlist
- Collection
- Personal Lists
- All Three

You can then choose a direction:
- Two-Way (Additive)
- Trakt -> PunchPlay
- PunchPlay -> Trakt

Transfers are deliberately additive: Finder adds missing items to the destination and does not remove existing items. Existing content is preserved. Personal Lists can be created on the destination when needed.

This is a manual transfer utility, not a background synchronization service.
