Finder is a customizable media browser for Movies, TV Shows, Seasons and Episodes.

Use Tools -> Tips for Use at any time to return to this guide. Finder's menus, context actions, trackers, list managers, scraping controls, playback features and optional Finder Skin integration are designed to be configured to suit your setup.

The most important starting choices are your Finder interface mode and Primary Tracker. Finder can operate as a standalone add-on or integrate directly with Finder Skin when that skin is active. Your Primary Tracker determines the watched/progress source used by Finder. See the dedicated tips for Finder Interface and Primary Tracker before changing related settings.
