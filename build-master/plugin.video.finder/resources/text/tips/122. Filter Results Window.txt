After Finder scrapes results, press Left while viewing the results to open the filter sideblade.

Depending on the available result data, you can filter by provider, quality and other result information. You can also filter by text:

Filter by Title
- Keeps results whose title contains the entered keyword(s).

Filter by Extra Info
- Keeps results whose Extra Info contains the entered keyword(s).

Use Back to leave the filter view and return to the complete result set.
