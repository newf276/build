1.0.05 — Phase 2 Finalization

- Finalized Finder-integrated TV widgets: Genres, Categories, In Progress, Popular Today, Recently Trending, Most Watched, Providers, and Networks.
- Finalized integrated Finder Search behavior and retained stable Search History/result navigation.
- Added Finder-native MDbList rating presentation for supported IMDb, IMDb Popular, Metacritic, Rotten Tomatoes critic/audience, and TMDb ratings.
- MDbList support in this release is ratings display only; it is not a watched provider or Primary Tracker.
- Retained Finder-only integration without a separate FENtastic/Finder helper add-on.
- Added Finder Skin Tips and a Finder-specific Setup Guide under Extra Info.
- Preserved accepted Finder home-menu appearance, category navigation, widget presentation, and Search information layout.
- Public release numbering synchronized with Finder 1.0.05 for GitHub/repository continuity.
